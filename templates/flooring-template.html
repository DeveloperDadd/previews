Our Services

Complete Hardwood Flooring Services

Beyond refinishing, Your Flooring Co. offers a full range of hardwood flooring services for homeowners across Your County. Whatever your floors need, our team brings the expertise and craftsmanship to deliver exceptional results.

Custom Staining

From natural tones to rich espresso, we test stain samples right on your floor so you can see exactly how the color looks in your light before we commit.

Hardwood Floor Sanding

Professional dust-controlled sanding removes old finish, stains and surface damage, leaving a smooth, even surface ready for a fresh finish.

Hardwood Floor Repair

Damaged, warped or missing boards are replaced and woven in seamlessly, so repairs blend in with the rest of your floor.

Full Floor Restoration

Complete restoration of neglected or heavily worn floors: deep repairs, sanding, staining and multiple finish coats to bring them back to life.
