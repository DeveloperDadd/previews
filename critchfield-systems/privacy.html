Privacy Policy

How Critchfield Systems collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated November 24, 2026.

Who we are

Critchfield Systems (“we,” “us” or “our”) is a web design business based in Lexington, Kentucky. We build and host websites for small businesses and serve customers across the United States remotely. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit critchfieldsystems.com or contact us, and how we use it. Questions? Call or text (859) 270-7050 or email critchfieldsystems@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us. This website does not have a sign-up or contact form. We receive information when you call, text or email us, and when you become a customer. That can include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your business name, services, service area and hours
  • Photos, logos, videos and text you send us to put on your website
  • The content of your messages, and billing details needed to charge you for your plan

Information collected automatically. When you visit this website, we and our service providers receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, the pages you view, how long you stay, the website that referred you, and your approximate location (for example, city or region) based on your IP address. With our session-recording tool (described below), we also see how you interact with pages, such as clicks, taps, scrolling and mouse movement.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your calls, texts and emails
  • To build, host, secure and maintain your website
  • To bill you and keep records
  • To understand how people use this website so we can fix problems and make it faster and clearer
  • To protect against fraud, abuse and security threats, and to meet legal obligations

Analytics, cookies and similar tools

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics for basic traffic statistics. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as privacy-focused, and it does not use cookies to track visitors. Cloudflare also processes visitor IP addresses as part of delivering and securing the site. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity is a free analytics tool that records how visitors use this website, including session replays and heatmaps. Clarity uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize a visit across pages, and it masks sensitive content by default. Microsoft receives and processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement and the Clarity terms.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface used on this website. When a page loads, your browser requests the font from Google, which means Google receives your IP address. See the Google Privacy Policy.

You can limit cookies and tracking through your browser settings, a content blocker or a privacy-focused browser. Blocking these tools will not stop you from using the website.

Text messages

If you text us, we use your phone number and message only to reply and to provide our services. We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in information with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. If we ever send you recurring or automated text messages, we will first get your consent, tell you how often to expect messages, and you can reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as hosting, analytics, payment processing, email and phone services. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety recipients when we believe disclosure is required by law, or necessary to protect our rights, our customers or others.
  • A successor if our business is sold or transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

If you are a customer, the business information and content you ask us to publish on your website becomes public when the site goes live.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, to keep business and tax records, and to resolve disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it. Analytics providers keep data under their own retention periods.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by calling, texting or emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law. We will respond to reasonable requests as required by law. We may need to verify your identity first.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS, and we use reputable providers to host and protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Continuing to use the website after an update means you accept the updated policy.
