Services

Dr. Peterson is an oral and maxillofacial surgeon. His work ranges from a single tooth that has to come out to surgery on the jaw itself.

  • Wisdom teeth removal

    One tooth or all four, including teeth that have to be cut out.

  • Tooth extractions

    A single tooth or several in one visit.

  • Dental implants

    A post placed in the jaw to hold a replacement tooth.

  • Bone grafts

    Rebuilding bone in the jaw, often so an implant has a solid base.

  • Denture-preparation surgery

    Reshaping the ridge of the jaw so dentures sit right.

  • Corrective jaw surgery

    Orthognathic surgery to fix how the upper and lower jaws line up.

  • Dental anesthesia

    Numbing or anesthesia, depending on the procedure. Ask about your options when you call.

About Dr. Peterson

Dr. Gilman Peterson earned his dental degree (DMD) and his medical degree (MD) at the University of Kentucky, then finished his residency in oral and maxillofacial surgery there too. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery.

New patients

A few things to know before your first appointment.

  1. 1

    Call the office

    Appointments are recommended, so call (833) 546-3522 to get on the schedule. Have your insurance card ready.

  2. 2

    Fill out your forms ahead

    New patient forms can be filled out before your visit, which shortens check-in. Ask about them when you call.

  3. 3

    Come back for a follow-up

    After an extraction you come back for a short follow-up visit so Dr. Peterson can check how you're healing.
